Cleaning After Renovation, Repair, or Construction Work

Construction dust has a way of settling everywhere: counters, trim, doors, bathrooms, kitchens, floors, windowsills, vents, light fixtures, and high-touch areas. Our post-construction cleaning is built to bring the space back to a clean, presentable, move-in-ready or guest-ready condition.

This service is a strong fit after remodels, flooring work, cabinet installation, painting touch-ups, handyman projects, rental repairs, punch-list work, and new project turnover where the space needs careful cleaning before use.

Dust and Surface Reset

Reachable horizontal surfaces, doors, trim, baseboards, fixtures, and detail areas are wiped to help remove fine construction dust.

Kitchens and Bathrooms

Sinks, counters, cabinets, fixtures, mirrors, toilets, tubs, showers, and visible surfaces are cleaned so the most-used rooms feel fresh.

Floors and Presentation

Floors are vacuumed, swept, and mopped based on the surface type, with a final walkthrough focused on overall shine and readiness.

Post-Construction Cleaning FAQ

What is included in post-construction cleaning?
Post-construction cleaning can include dust removal, wiping reachable surfaces, cleaning kitchens and bathrooms, floors, fixtures, baseboards, doors, and detail areas affected by construction or renovation dust.
Do you remove construction debris?
Cleaning Heroes focuses on cleaning and presentation. Large debris removal, hauling, hazardous materials, paint removal, and specialty restoration are not included unless agreed to in writing.
Do you clean after renovations in rental properties?
Yes. We can help clean vacation rentals, second homes, residential homes, and commercial spaces after repairs, renovations, or installation work.
