Cleaning After the Work Is Done

Construction and renovation dust can settle on almost everything, even after the major work is finished. We focus on the details that make a space feel usable again: surfaces, trim, fixtures, kitchens, bathrooms, floors, doors, ledges, and presentation areas.

This service is ideal after remodeling, flooring, cabinet work, painting touch-ups, punch-list repairs, handyman projects, new construction, rental refreshes, and commercial build-outs.

Dust Detail

Reachable surfaces, baseboards, doors, trim, light fixtures, ledges, and common touchpoints are wiped to reduce fine construction dust.

Kitchen and Bath Reset

Counters, sinks, fixtures, cabinet exteriors, mirrors, toilets, showers, tubs, and visible surfaces are cleaned for a finished feel.

Floors and Final Pass

Floors are vacuumed, swept, and mopped based on the material, followed by a walkthrough focused on presentation.

Post-Construction Cleaning FAQ

Who provides post-construction cleaning in Pawleys Island?
Cleaning Heroes provides post-construction cleaning in Pawleys Island and nearby coastal communities including Litchfield, Murrells Inlet, Georgetown, Garden City, Surfside Beach, and Myrtle Beach.
What does post-construction cleaning include?
Post-construction cleaning can include dust removal, wiping reachable surfaces, cleaning kitchens and bathrooms, vacuuming and mopping floors, detail cleaning trim and fixtures, and preparing the property for residents, guests, showings, or business use.
Can Cleaning Heroes clean after a renovation or remodel?
Yes. Cleaning Heroes cleans after renovations, remodels, repairs, flooring work, cabinet installation, painting touch-ups, handyman projects, and punch-list work where dust and presentation matter.
Is post-construction cleaning different from deep cleaning?
Yes. Deep cleaning focuses on buildup from normal use, while post-construction cleaning focuses on renovation dust, project residue, fixtures, trim, floors, and final presentation after construction or repair work.
